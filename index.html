Band Score Criteria

Fluency & Coherence

Speak at length without noticeable hesitation. Use a range of cohesive devices. Relevance and logical organization of ideas.

Lexical Resource

Wide vocabulary range. Awareness of style and collocation. Skillfully use uncommon vocabulary. Discuss topics with precision.

Grammatical Range

Wide range of structures. Majority of sentences are error-free. Good control of complex structures. Grammatical mistakes are rare.

Pronunciation

Clear intonation and stress patterns. Easy to understand throughout. Good use of features like chunking and linking.
